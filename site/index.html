A fast desktop music player for the music you own.

Soft, liquid and lit by your album covers. Instant search, gapless playback, visualizers that move with the music, and every listen remembered. Entirely yours: no account, no cloud, no ads.

{{WAVE}}

Everything your library deserves

Built for people with a folder full of music they love, from a few albums to fifty thousand tracks.

A look of its own

Frosted glass over colours that drift from the album that's playing, a floating player, and a seek bar drawn from each song's own waveform.

Now Playing, full screen

The cover, or the record sliding out of its sleeve, over Liquid, Aurora and Nebula visualizers that move with the music in the cover's colours.

Browse like a record shelf

Album and artist pages with liner notes, a colour wall that sorts every cover round the colour wheel, a timeline by decade and year, and your own folders.

Every listen remembered

A searchable history by day with a year-long heatmap and streaks, a recap of every week, month and year, and what you played on this day in past years.

Gapless and honest

True gapless playback (CUE sheet albums too), ReplayGain measured where tags are missing, headphone correction from AutoEQ and crossfeed, and a signal path readout that tells you when the sound is untouched.

Radio and smart playlists

Similar songs from your own library keep playing when the queue runs out, and playlists build themselves from rules. Coming from another player? Your play counts, ratings and playlists come with you.

Looks after your library

Instant search across fifty thousand tracks, Ctrl+K for anything, Ctrl/Shift+click to act on many songs at once, and a health check that finds missing covers, tags and duplicates, with a fix next to each.

Private by design

Your library, playlists and listening history stay on your computer. Scrobbling to Last.fm or ListenBrainz and Discord are strictly opt-in, and offline mode turns off every internet connection with one switch.

An AppImage that runs on any modern x86-64 distribution, with everything bundled.

Download AppImage

Make it executable, then run it: chmod +x Hallucinate-x86_64.AppImage. {{LINUX_MORE}}

macOS

A native app for Apple silicon Macs (M1 and newer).

Download for Mac

Unzip and move Hallucinate to Applications. It isn't notarized yet, so if macOS blocks it the first time, allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
