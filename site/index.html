A fast desktop music player for the music you own.

Soft, liquid and lit by your album covers. Instant search, gapless playback, visualizers that move with the music, and every listen remembered. Entirely yours: no account, no cloud, no ads.

{{WAVE}}

Everything your library deserves

Built for people with a folder full of music they love, from a few albums to a quarter of a million songs.

A look of its own

Frosted glass over colours that drift from the album that's playing, a floating player, and a seek bar drawn from each song's own waveform. Eighteen themes, any accent colour, or a theme of your own.

Now Playing, full screen

The cover, or the record sliding out of its sleeve, over Liquid, Aurora, Nebula, Halo and Waves visualizers that move with the music in the cover's colours.

Browse like a record shelf

Album and artist pages with liner notes, a Genres page with every song of each genre, composer pages (classical works with their movements in order), a colour wall that sorts every cover round the colour wheel, a timeline by decade and year, and your own folders.

Every listen remembered

A searchable history by day with a year-long heatmap and streaks, a recap of every week, month and year, and what you played on this day in past years.

Gapless and honest

True gapless playback (CUE sheet albums too), ReplayGain measured where tags are missing, headphone correction from AutoEQ and crossfeed, a signal path readout that tells you when the sound is untouched, and a spectrum analyzer to see what a file really holds.

Radio, podcasts, your server

Internet radio, podcasts and your Jellyfin or Subsonic server, off until you want them. Radio mode keeps the queue going from your own library, and smart playlists build themselves. Coming from another player? Your play counts, ratings and playlists come with you.

Looks after your library

Instant search across a quarter of a million songs that forgives a typo, Ctrl+K for anything, Ctrl/Shift+click to act on many songs at once, and a health check that finds missing covers, tags and duplicates, with a fix next to each. Album tags can come from MusicBrainz in one search.

Private by design

Your library, playlists and listening history stay on your computer, and there's no telemetry. Scrobbling to Last.fm or ListenBrainz and Discord are strictly opt-in, and offline mode turns off every internet connection with one switch. Back it all up to one file and restore it anywhere.

Free forever. Pick your platform.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Media keys and the system media overlay work out of the box.

Download installer

Not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn: choose More info → Run anyway. A portable zip is also available.

Linux

An AppImage that runs on any modern x86-64 distribution, with everything bundled. Or a .deb or .rpm for Debian, Ubuntu, Fedora and openSUSE, or nix run github:Phosphene-dev/Hallucinate.

Download AppImage

Make it executable, then run it: chmod +x Hallucinate-x86_64.AppImage. {{LINUX_MORE}}

macOS

A native app for Apple silicon Macs (M1 and newer).

Download for Mac

Unzip and move Hallucinate to Applications. It isn't notarized yet, so if macOS blocks it the first time, allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
